The Sweetness of Nabat

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer for Persian weddings?

We provide bilingual (Farsi & English) wedding officiating, custom ceremony scriptwriting, Sofreh Aghd guidance and traditions, and seamless coordination for multicultural weddings.

Can the ceremony be performed in both Farsi and English?

Yes! Elmira Jafari specializes in bilingual ceremonies that transition effortlessly between Farsi and English, ensuring both family generations and non-Persian guests feel fully included.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes, Elmira frequently travels across North America (including California, New York, Florida, Texas, Illinois, and Canada) as well as international destination weddings worldwide.

Can we customize our wedding script and readings?

Absolutely. We believe your ceremony should reflect your unique love story. Elmira works closely with you to customize every aspect of the script, blending traditional Persian poetry (like Hafez, Saadi, and Rumi) with modern wedding scripts.

Where are your main service locations?

While Elmira is local to the Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia (DMV) area, she regularly travels to perform ceremonies in Texas (Houston, Dallas, Austin), Illinois (Chicago), New York (NYC), California (LA, SF, Orange County), Florida (Miami), and for destination weddings worldwide.

What is the meaning of "Nabat" in a Persian wedding?

In a traditional Persian wedding (Sofreh Aghd), Nabat (sugar candy) symbolizes sweetness, joy, and happy beginnings for the couple's shared life ahead.
