The Sweetness of Nabat

In a traditional Persian Sofreh Aghd, Nabat (sugar candy) represents the sweet beginnings of a shared life. It is the cornerstone of love, joy, and the journey ahead.

Meet Elmira Jafari

With over 15 years of experience as an award-nominated international journalist and documentary filmmaker, Elmira brings professional poise, voice control, and a deep reverence for cultural storytelling to your wedding ceremony.

Now local to the DMV area, she is deeply inspired by the poetry of Hafez, Saadi, and Rumi, crafting ceremonies that are beautifully tailored, culturally rich, and elegantly modern. With a Master's degree in communication and a background in visual arts, she holds space for your love story with grace and warmth.
