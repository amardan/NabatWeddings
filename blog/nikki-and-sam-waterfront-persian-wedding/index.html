Behind the Scenes: How Preparation & Familiarity Created a Flawless Wedding Day for Nikki & Sam

By Elmira Jafari

For couples planning their dream wedding, one priority stands above all else: complete peace of mind. When your wedding day arrives, you want to step up to the aisle knowing that every cue, every word, and every family tradition will unfold with effortless grace.

On August 8, 2026, Nikki & Sam celebrated their breathtaking destination waterfront Persian wedding. But the magic of that moment wasn't an accident—it was the result of weeks of thoughtful preparation, familiar collaboration, and an on-site rehearsal the day before.

✨ What Gives Our Couples Total Peace of Mind

  • Weeks of Personal Consultation: Collaborative 1-on-1 meetings to craft bespoke Farsi & English vows, poetry, and custom ceremony scripts.
  • Early On-Site Travel: Arriving a full day early to manage venue logistics and integrate audio seamlessly with planners & DJs.
  • In-Person Rehearsal Walkthrough: Leading the rehearsal with the couple, wedding party, and families so everyone feels comfortable and calm.
  • Warm, Familiar Execution: Delivering a ceremony that feels deeply personal, intimate, and engaging for every single guest.

1. Weeks Before: Crafting a Bespoke Bilingual Script

Long before traveling to the venue, the journey begins with 1-on-1 planning calls. Weeks in advance, Elmira collaborates closely with the couple to design a ceremony text that reflects their unique love story.

Every word, oath, and classical Persian poem is curated with care. Handwritten annotations mark rhythm, pauses, and translation transitions so that both Farsi-speaking and English-speaking families feel equally honored and connected.

Elmira's annotated bilingual script blending Hafez poetry with modern vows.

2. Rehearsal Day: On-Site Early to Guarantee Smoothness

For destination weddings, arriving early is paramount. Elmira traveled to the venue a full day prior to the wedding, staying on-site to ensure complete coordination.

On Rehearsal Day, Elmira led a comprehensive walkthrough with Nikki, Sam, their wedding party, parents, and venue staff. Going over processional timing, entrance cues, and the pacing around the Sofreh Aghd eliminated all wedding-day jitters.

Elmira Jafari on Rehearsal Day—holding the ceremony binder and ensuring every detail is rehearsed.

Behind the scenes: Rehearsal walkthrough with the couple and wedding party.

3. Wedding Day: Total Calm & Celebration

Because every detail had been rehearsed and verified, wedding morning was filled with pure joy. Set on an open-air deck overlooking the waterfront marina, the circular Sofreh Aghd stood ready amidst fresh roses and greenery.

Standing at the podium, Elmira delivered the bilingual ceremony with calm authority, warmth, and storytelling mastery—guiding Nikki & Sam seamlessly into married life.
