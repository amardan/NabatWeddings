Behind the Scenes: Crafting a Destination Waterfront Persian Wedding for Nikki & Sam

By Elmira Jafari

Every wedding ceremony is a distinct story waiting to be told, but destination celebrations carry a unique spark of excitement. On August 8, 2026, two beautiful souls—Nikki & Sam—brought their families and friends together for an unforgettable waterfront Persian wedding. Behind every seamless ceremony lies hours of quiet preparation, intentional scriptwriting, and dedicated travel. Today, we take you behind the scenes to show how a dream ceremony transitions from script to reality.

The Journey & Preparation

For destination weddings, preparation begins long before arriving at the venue. From packing ceremonial garments to organizing travel logistics, every detail is carefully coordinated so that on the wedding morning, our couples feel complete calm and confidence.

Elmira Jafari preparing prior to officiating Nikki & Sam's ceremony.

Crafting a Bespoke Bilingual Script

One of the core hallmarks of a Nabat Persian Wedding is our seamless flow between Farsi and English. In the hotel suite hours before the ceremony, final script revisions take place—weaving classical Persian poetry alongside modern English vows. With handwritten annotations marking rhythm, pauses, and cultural cues, the script ensures every guest, regardless of background, feels deeply included in the sacred ritual.

Annotated bilingual script blending classical Persian poetry with modern English vows.

The Ceremony: Open-Air Waterfront Sofreh Aghd

Set upon an open wooden deck directly overlooking pristine blue waters and docked boats, Nikki & Sam's ceremony space was breathtaking. At the center sat a lush, circular Sofreh Aghd adorned with fresh white roses, fragrant greenery, and classic symbolic elements reflecting sweetness, light, and prosperity.

As guests gathered under the clear summer sky, Elmira Jafari took her place at the podium. With warm vocal projection and respectful command of Persian traditions, the ceremony moved gracefully through the poetic readings, the traditional asking of consent (Aghd), and the exchange of vows.

Nikki & Sam's waterfront ceremony in full celebration.
