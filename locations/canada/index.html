Canada's Premier Bilingual Persian Wedding Officiant

Canada is home to one of the most prominent and vibrant Persian diaspora populations outside of Iran. Elmira Jafari travels regularly to Canada—focusing especially on Toronto and the GTA—to officiate elegant bilingual Farsi & English wedding ceremonies. She bridges time-honored Iranian customs with the narrative of modern couples.

Services Available in Canada

  • Bilingual Farsi & English Ceremonies — Beautifully tailored to engage both Farsi-speaking families and diverse English-speaking guests
  • Sofreh Aghd Coordination & Officiating — Seamless presentation of classic symbols (Aayeneh, Shamdan, Noon-o-Paneer) combined with reciting classical poetry
  • Custom Wedding Scripts — Custom Farsi-English vow exchanges and storytelling structured to make your ceremony feel uniquely personal
  • Travel & Multi-Day Wedding Coordination — Experienced in destination wedding planning and ceremony timelines

Optimized for Canada's Rich Persian Heritage

For Persian-Canadians in Ontario and British Columbia, weddings are grand celebrations that require a deep appreciation of cultural legacy. Elmira brings her background in broadcasting, media, and professional storytelling to deliver ceremonies with the elegance, rhythm, and respect your families deserve.

In Toronto (specifically Richmond Hill, Vaughan, and North York), families value authentic representation. Elmira recites classic Persian love poetry by Hafez, Rumi, and Saadi, and translates the traditional meanings of the Sofreh Aghd items in a way that moves guests of all backgrounds.

Whether you are hosting a luxury indoor banquet, a chic downtown loft wedding in Toronto, or an outdoor mountain-view ceremony in Vancouver, Elmira creates a memorable experience.
