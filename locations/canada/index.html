Canada's Premier Bilingual Persian & Farsi Officiant

Canada is home to one of the most vibrant Persian populations outside of Iran. Elmira Jafari travels regularly across Canada—focusing especially on Toronto (GTA) and Vancouver—to officiate elegant bilingual Farsi & English wedding ceremonies. She bridges time-honored Iranian customs with modern love stories.

Services Available in Canada

  • Bilingual Farsi & English Ceremonies — Beautifully tailored to engage both Farsi-speaking families and diverse English-speaking guests.
  • Sofreh Aghd Officiating — Seamless presentation of classic symbols (Aayeneh, Shamdan, Nabat) combined with reciting classical poetry.
  • Custom Wedding Scripts — Custom Farsi-English vow exchanges and storytelling structured to make your ceremony uniquely personal.
  • International Destination Travel — Dedicated travel packages for Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, and international destination weddings.

Optimized for Canada's Rich Persian Heritage

For Persian-Canadians in Ontario and British Columbia, weddings are grand celebrations requiring deep appreciation of cultural legacy. Elmira brings her background in broadcasting, media, and professional storytelling to deliver ceremonies with dignity and warmth.

In Toronto (specifically Richmond Hill, Vaughan, and North York), families value authentic representation. Elmira recites classic Persian love poetry by Hafez, Rumi, and Saadi, translating traditional Sofreh Aghd meanings so guests of all backgrounds feel moved.

Whether you are hosting a luxury indoor banquet in Toronto or a mountain-view ceremony in Vancouver, Elmira creates an unforgettable experience.
