Why NYC Couples Choose Nabat Persian Weddings

New York's Persian couples seek officiants who understand both tradition and the cosmopolitan nature of their celebrations. Elmira brings that perfect balance with her international background and professional storytelling expertise.

From elegant Manhattan hotels like The Plaza and The Pierre to waterfront Brooklyn venues and Long Island estates, Elmira has the experience to deliver flawless ceremonies in any setting.

Her deep knowledge of Persian poetry and customs, combined with fluent bilingual delivery, ensures every guest connects with your ceremony, whether they speak Farsi, English, or both.
