Florida Marriage Licensing & Out-of-State Waiver

Jurisdiction & Clerk: In Florida, marriage licenses are issued by the Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller in any county (such as Miami-Dade County Clerk or Palm Beach County Clerk).

Waiting Period Rules: Florida has a 3-day waiting period for Florida residents (waived if a premarital preparation course is completed). However, non-Florida residents have ZERO waiting period—the license is effective immediately upon issuance!

Validity: Florida marriage licenses are valid for 60 calendar days anywhere within the State of Florida.

Officiant Signing: Elmira Jafari is legally authorized under Florida Statutes to solemnize marriages and will execute your marriage certificate immediately following your celebration.

How does Florida marriage licensing work for out-of-state and destination couples?
For couples traveling to Florida for a destination wedding who are non-Florida residents, Florida has zero waiting period. You can visit any Florida County Clerk of Courts office (such as Miami-Dade County Clerk), obtain your license in person, and be married the same day. The license is valid for 60 days. Elmira Jafari will sign and file your completed license with the county court.
