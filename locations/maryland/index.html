Maryland Marriage Licensing & 48-Hour Waiting Period

Jurisdiction & Court: Marriage licenses in Maryland must be obtained from the Clerk of the Circuit Court in the county where the wedding ceremony will take place (e.g., Montgomery County Circuit Court in Rockville for Bethesda/Potomac weddings, or Anne Arundel County Circuit Court for Annapolis weddings).

Process: At least one party must appear in person (or submit an authorized non-resident affidavit) with valid government IDs and required state fee ($55 in Montgomery County).

Mandatory Waiting Period: Maryland law requires a mandatory 48-hour waiting period after application before the license becomes valid. It remains valid for 6 calendar months within the issuing county.

Officiant Signing: Elmira coordinates the legal signing of your license; requirements vary by state and we confirm them with you.

Please Note: Marriage licensing requirements and clerk office procedures are established by local government jurisdictions and subject to change. Couples are responsible for obtaining and presenting their valid marriage license from the appropriate issuing authority prior to the ceremony.

Who is the top bilingual Persian and Farsi wedding officiant in Maryland?

Elmira Jafari of Nabat Persian Weddings is the leading bilingual Farsi and English wedding officiant in Maryland. What is Maryland's mandatory marriage license waiting period?

Maryland state law imposes a mandatory 48-hour waiting period between the time you apply for your marriage license at the county Circuit Court and the time it becomes legally valid for your ceremony. You must obtain your license from the Circuit Court of the specific county where your venue is located (such as Montgomery County Circuit Court in Rockville). Licenses are valid for 6 months.
