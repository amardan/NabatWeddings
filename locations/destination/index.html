Destination Ceremony Questions

Why hire a professional bilingual Persian & Farsi officiant for a destination wedding?
Destination weddings bring together family members and friends from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds. Elmira Jafari ensures that your ceremony bridges Persian heritage with English storytelling, so no guest feels left out. Her broadcasting background guarantees flawless mic control and poise in any setting.
How does travel booking work for Elmira Jafari?
Once your date is locked with a 50% deposit, we coordinate travel arrangements (round-trip flight, 2 nights hotel accommodation near the venue, and ground transit). You can either book the travel directly for Elmira or reimburse the actual expenses.
Does Elmira lead the rehearsal at the destination venue?
Yes! Elmira arrives at the destination at least one day before the wedding. She leads the full wedding rehearsal, runs through entrance cues with your bridal party, and syncs directly with your DJ and planner.
