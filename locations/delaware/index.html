Why Delaware Coast Couples Choose Nabat Persian Weddings

Delaware's coast hosts many couples from Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia who are seeking a beautiful retreat for their wedding day. They trust Elmira Jafari because of her close ties to the DMV area and her experience officiating destination wedding ceremonies.

Elmira's background in broadcasting and journalism ensures a professional vocal delivery that handles outdoor acoustics—like soft ocean waves and coastal wind—with ease, ensuring every guest hears and understands the beautiful translation of the ceremony.

From classical Persian poetry selections to the sweet traditions of the honey-sharing and sugar-showering, Elmira guides you and your family through the wedding day with exceptional care and organization.
