Pennsylvania Marriage Licensing & 3-Day Waiting Period

Jurisdiction & Clerk: Marriage licenses in Pennsylvania are issued by the Marriage License Bureau / Register of Wills in any PA county (e.g., Philadelphia City Hall, Room 413, Philadelphia, PA 19107).

Waiting Period & Validity: Pennsylvania enforces a mandatory 3-day waiting period from the date of application. The license is valid for 60 calendar days anywhere in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

Officiant Authority: Elmira Jafari is legally authorized to solemnize marriages throughout Pennsylvania and will sign and file your duplicate marriage certificate with the county court.
