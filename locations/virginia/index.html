Virginia Marriage Licensing & Legal Requirements

Jurisdiction & Court: In Virginia, marriage licenses are issued by the Clerk of the Circuit Court in any county or independent city (such as Fairfax County Circuit Court in Fairfax, Loudoun County Circuit Court in Leesburg, or Arlington Circuit Court).

Process: Both parties must appear in person before the clerk, present government-issued photo identification, and pay the state licensing fee ($30 cash or card depending on county).

Waiting Period & Validity: Virginia has zero mandatory waiting period. The license is valid immediately upon issuance and remains active for 60 calendar days within the Commonwealth of Virginia.

Officiant Authority: Elmira Jafari is an authorized and registered marriage celebrant in Virginia Circuit Courts. She signs and files your Virginia Marriage Register immediately following your ceremony.
