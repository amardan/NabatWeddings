Washington D.C. Marriage Licensing & Officiant Authorization

Jurisdiction & Court: Marriage licenses in the District of Columbia are issued by the Marriage Bureau of the Superior Court of the District of Columbia (Moultrie Courthouse, 500 Indiana Ave NW, Room 4485, Washington, D.C. 20001).

Process: Couples can apply online through the D.C. Courts portal or in person by appointment. Both parties must provide government photo IDs and legal proof of age.

Waiting Period & Validity: Washington D.C. has no mandatory waiting period once the application is processed, and D.C. marriage licenses do not expire.

Officiant Signing: Elmira Jafari is fully authorized and registered with the D.C. Superior Court to officiate legal civil and religious marriages in the District. Immediately following your ceremony, Elmira signs and files your official marriage certificate with the court.
