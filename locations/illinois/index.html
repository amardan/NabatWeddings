Cook County / Illinois Marriage Licensing Rules

Jurisdiction & Clerk: Marriage licenses in Illinois must be obtained from the County Clerk of the county where the ceremony takes place (e.g., Cook County Clerk's Vital Records Office in Downtown Chicago or suburban branch locations).

Waiting Period & Validity: Illinois requires a 1-day (24-hour) waiting period—the license becomes effective the calendar day AFTER it is issued. It is valid for 60 calendar days only within the issuing county.

Officiant Authority: Elmira Jafari is legally recognized to perform civil and religious marriages in Illinois and will sign and file your marriage certificate with the Cook County Clerk following the ceremony.

How does marriage licensing work in Chicago and Cook County?
Couples getting married in Chicago must obtain their license from the Cook County Clerk's office (such as 118 N. Clark St in downtown Chicago). Both parties must appear in person. Illinois has a 1-day waiting period (the license is effective the day after issuance) and is valid for 60 days in Cook County. Elmira will complete the legal signing after your ceremony.
