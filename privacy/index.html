Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you inquire about our services or book a ceremony, we may collect:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Wedding details (date, location, venue information)
  • Personal preferences for ceremony customization
  • Payment information for services rendered

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer service
  • Plan and execute your wedding ceremony
  • Send relevant updates about your booking
  • Improve our services and website experience

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers (such as payment processors) who assist us in operating our business, under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses analytics tools (such as Vercel Web Analytics) to understand how visitors interact with our site. This data is collected anonymously and used solely to improve user experience.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
