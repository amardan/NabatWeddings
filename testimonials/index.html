Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Elmira Jafari and what is Nabat Persian Weddings?

Elmira Jafari is a professional, bilingual (Farsi & English) Persian wedding officiant serving the DMV (DC, Maryland, Virginia) area and worldwide. "Nabat Persian Weddings" specializes in modern and traditional Sofreh Aghd wedding ceremonies, bringing professional voice control and poetic storytelling to your big day.

Do you perform ceremonies in both English and Farsi?

Yes! Every ceremony is custom-crafted to flow naturally between Farsi and English. This ensures all your guests, from family elders who speak Farsi to English-speaking friends, feel completely engaged, included, and touched by the ceremony.

Can we customize the Sofreh Aghd script and our wedding vows?

Absolutely. We believe your ceremony should reflect your unique love story. Elmira works closely with you to customize every aspect of the script, blending traditional Persian poetry (like Hafez, Saadi, and Rumi) with modern wedding vows.

Where are your main service locations?

While Elmira is local to the Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia (DMV) area, she regularly travels to perform ceremonies in New York (NYC), California (LA, SF, Orange County), Florida (Miami), Texas (Houston, Dallas), and for destination weddings worldwide.

What is the meaning of "Nabat" in a Persian wedding?

In a traditional Persian wedding (Sofreh Aghd), Nabat (sugar candy) symbolizes sweetness, joy, and happy beginnings for the couple's shared life ahead.
